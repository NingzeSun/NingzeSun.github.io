Research

I'm interested in machine learning and robotics. My current work is on bimanual manipulation with decentralized policies: each arm acts on its own observation and a compact message from its partner.

Bi-SKAD: Bimanual Semantic-Kinematic Affordance Diffusion
Ningze Sun, Roya Firoozi
2026
project page

Two per-arm diffusion policies, trained together and coordinated through a compact message at execution, keep working when one arm's sensing fails or its motion is delayed.

Ray-Traced Augmentation for Signal Strength Based Localization
Jihoon Og, Ningze Sun, Ioanis Nikolaidis, Omid Ardakanian
arXiv, 2026
arXiv / PDF

Calibrated ray tracing on a building model generates synthetic Wi-Fi fingerprints. A localizer trained only on them reaches 3.05 m mean error in a real campus building, 33.6% lower than the best baseline.
